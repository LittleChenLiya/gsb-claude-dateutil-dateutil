Added :mod:`dateutil.schedule`, an adapter that turns a local
:mod:`dateutil.rrule` recurrence rule into ordered, de-duplicated UTC
instants over a UTC half-open interval.  Imaginary wall-clock times
(spring-forward gaps) can be skipped or shifted forward, and ambiguous
wall-clock times (fall-back overlaps) can resolve to the earlier offset,
the later offset, or both.  ``COUNT`` and ``UNTIL`` limit the number of
rule candidates, not the number of output instants.
